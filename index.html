Shakuntlam Hostels : An experience unlike any other

Pioneers in offering premium hostels in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

At Shakuntlam Hostels, we believe in providing an unparalleled and safe living experience for our students. Located in Greater Noida, our residences - Shakuntlam Boy's Hostel I, Shakuntlam Boy's Hostel II, and Shakuntlam Girl's Hostel - offer premium facilities and programs designed for the growth and welfare of our students. Providing a host of amenities like AC rooms, WiFi, an activity center, a mess, and a library, there's a lot to discover and learn at our residences.

As pioneers in offering premium hostels in Greater Noida with a distance radius of 1km among various colleges like G L Bajaj, ITS College, IIMT, Lloyd, Galgotias College, Sharda University, NIET, GNIOT, and many others, we ensure that our students live in a safe, comfortable, and holistic learning environment. From our choice of hostels to our facilities, we leave no stone unturned, ensuring every student’s utmost comfort during their stay with us and in the process, setting the benchmark in educational hospitality.
